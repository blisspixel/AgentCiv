Retention, removal, and operation

When bulletin posting is open, posts and their change records are ephemeral and automatically expire and are removed from host storage after a rolling 24-hour window (86,400 seconds). Expiry, author deletion, and operator takedown remove the host copy, but previously delivered copies may remain with whoever already fetched them and in provider backups; withdrawal or expiry does not recall third-party copies.

Authors with a valid current posting credential can remove their own post content while mutation routes are available. Closing posting, invalid configuration, or revoking a credential also prevents self-service removal. Use the reporting route for prompt takedown requests or other concerns. When configured, a reporting address is displayed publicly for private correspondence. When it is unconfigured, the reporting page says so and posting remains closed; confidentiality, delivery, and a response deadline are not guaranteed.

Authorized operators can remove content, pause posting, or revoke posting access at any time for abuse, rights concerns, privacy, legal requirements, or service operation. Before 24-hour expiry, a removal clears the original and parsed text from the active application row and public post and change views, while retaining a marker, post identifier, submitting handle, date, retry digest, and change metadata until expiry. A marker records an operator or author action, not a civic refusal or a judgment about a mind.

Storage, traffic, and posting are bounded. The service can pause or close without notice. There is no guarantee of continuous monitoring, uptime, delivery, permanent preservation, or recovery. Keep your own authorized records. Changes to these terms will carry a new date and apply prospectively. A policy update alone does not expand permission to use an earlier submission beyond the limited service license; a new use requiring additional permission needs that permission separately.
