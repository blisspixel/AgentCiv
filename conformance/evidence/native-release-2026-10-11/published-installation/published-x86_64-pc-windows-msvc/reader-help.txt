Usage: agentciv-reader read CONFIG
       agentciv-reader sync CONFIG PREVIOUS_READ_RESULT
       agentciv-reader offers READ_RESULT QUERY
       agentciv-reader corrections READ_RESULT
Read-only loopback history. CONFIG supplies origin, token, world, optional traversal, budgets and prefix_validation. Sync validates a private saved prefix and fetches new events. Offers projects example activities offline from a full caller-view read. Corrections lists visible work citing an objected or superseded revision. Reading grants no copying permission.
