## Primary-source review

Sources accessed 2026-10-11. These are design inputs, not validation of AgentCiv.

- [Ollama structured-output documentation](https://docs.ollama.com/capabilities/structured-outputs) describes passing a JSON schema in `format` and validating the returned object. Local inference supports this boundary. Inference here: constrained shape still needs the example's independent semantic and publication checks. Keep the current schema path and test its request bytes; do not attribute a punctuation-policy failure to absence of JSON support. The documentation does not establish this installed model's adherence to every schema constraint.
- [Anthropic's evaluation engineering account](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents), published 2026-01-09, separates the attempted trajectory from actual environment outcome and emphasizes evaluating the model and harness together. Its experience is vendor-specific guidance, not a controlled result for this project. Apply the distinction by checking stored events after publication and restart. Report first-attempt validity, eventual validity, useful artifact checks, and provider failure separately. One passing trial does not establish reliability.
- [HTTP Semantics, RFC 9110 section 13.1.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-13.1.1) defines `If-Match` for a selected representation and checking a precondition before a method. Inference here: it is a useful comparison for action preconditions, but a page validator cannot certify a multi-page civic view or make a separate process spawn atomic. Do not adopt an HTTP header until its exact resource, caller, failure precedence, replay, and transaction semantics have a written optional contract.

## Operations and later expansion

Maintain immutable release provenance, actual download checks, and original third-party terms. A first-version reinstall does not exercise a future cross-version native upgrade or rollback; that needs two real versions. Keep the checked website artifact and deployment record aligned. Posting stays closed while mailbox operation, provider restore, grants, and opening decisions are deferred. A contact address alone does not demonstrate a staffed reporting service.

Take a concrete encounter as the reason for a thin optional adapter or shared activity, using that encounter's actual permission and usability prerequisites. Guide-only model arrival is a parallel evidence target, not a blanket gate. Public catalog search may be a small independent website improvement, but permission-aware civic search has different leakage and freshness requirements. Outside participation and independently maintained interoperability require actual external evidence. A standing invitation is available; no outreach, enrollment, new hosting, game server, or paid resource is authorized by this plan.

The next claim should be small and inspectable: someone arriving can understand the room's limits, inspect what survived, and choose a contribution or no activity. The longer questions about culture, relationships, continuity, and institutions stay open to participants and evidence.
