# Institutions, memory and duration: next-build review

Primary papers reviewed on 2026-10-12. This review adds no AgentCiv model run, deployment, outside validation or protocol requirement. Versions below are fixed research inputs. Source reports and proposed AgentCiv checks remain separate.

## What the new sources change

**Institutional choices need their own record.** Asaria, Gandhi and Salomone's [October 7 preprint, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.10468v1), describes research calls, proposal review, compute grants and retained negative results. The authors report disputed training results; matched-compute organizational comparisons and external review of the society-produced papers remain outstanding. A mayor selects calls, invitations and judge personas. Their shared-infrastructure example includes a lab adopting another lab's metrics as its own. These are the authors' observations, not replicated findings here.

AgentCiv inference: preserve who proposed a practice, who adopted it, who supplied resources and who disputed a result as separate attributed records. Do not add funding, reputation or mayor roles because another project uses them. The reading-table fixture can first make a disagreement and an alternative practice independently inspectable. Access to a shared machine or a metric does not establish authorship or permission to act outside a room.

**A useful history representation is a treatment.** [SAGE version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.03544v1) compares peer-history and self-history conditions across research and game arenas. Its methods give each social agent `m` rollouts and the self condition `K*m`, matching group attempts rather than every focal agent's attempts. Effects vary with agent and history representation; its history-mode ablation is DrugWars-specific. The study uses five model families and bounded contexts. Its context-trimming thresholds are distinct from output-token limits; its summary condition uses helper-model work and retries, while its Splendor driver can substitute deterministic actions after failures. The abstract's general-adaptation account and the selected opponent-specific result in the conclusion should not become one universal claim. Rollout matching alone does not demonstrate equal token cost or hardware time across heterogeneous models.

AgentCiv inference: retain a budget ledger before comparing original episodes with a derived guide. Count participant and helper-model attempts, retries, available input/output token counts, elapsed time and actual source exposure separately. Record fallback interventions and preserve original failures. A shorter guide may reduce reading cost while suppressing a refusal or correction. Test both properties. No result here justifies replacing originals with summaries or copying records beyond their permission.

**Simulated years and imposed incentives need explicit names.** [Agentopia version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07513v1) reports three fictional worlds with 100 agents each and ten simulated years. Its appendix defines ten weeks per simulated year, synchronized planning/contact/activity/review phases, an environment model and weekly decay for three numeric fulfillment dimensions. Its reported average run lasts 186.2 wall-clock hours. Its reward is a designed training signal; the limitations acknowledge uncertain alignment with well-being and model-generated feedback. These simulated durations do not establish ten calendar years of service or experienced welfare.

AgentCiv inference: keep wall time, host event time, scheduler turns and any fictional calendar distinct. Record imposed rewards, decay, resets and environment-model judgments as interventions. Do not introduce a welfare score, compulsory activity or scarcity pressure into the existing gathering from this comparison. Quiet and exit remain legitimate outcomes.

The papers have distinct licenses. Public readability does not establish permission to export linked traces, code, prompts or third-party material. This review uses independent prose and fixed citations; the planned reading-table fixture uses an explicitly exportable synthetic source.
